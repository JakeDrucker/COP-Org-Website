Accounting Club

Accounting Club works to connect students potentially interested in accounting with recruiters from top public accounting firms, finance and internal accounting departments from industry leading companies, and banking/regulatory institutions to help students learn more about what a career in each field looks like, network with experienced professionals, and to hear about both internship and full-time opportunities at these companies. Alongside professional events, Accounting Club hosts both social and philanthropy events to help students get to know other students interested in accounting as well as to give back to the Champaign-Urbana community. Accounting Club is open to all majors (Gies & non-Gies) and there are no essays or interviews involved in becoming a member of the club. Please attend one or both of our info nights listed below to learn more about the club and how to join as well to hear from our corporate partners who will be presenting alongside our club. Info night attendance is not required to become a member, however, it’s a great opportunity to get answers to questions you may have about the club and to hear from top companies that are hiring accounting students.

Recruitment: "Info Night 1: EY | Wednesday August 26th, 6:00 PM | David Kinley Hall Room 141 | Business Casual Attire Info Night 2: BDO | Thursday August 27th, 6:00 PM | David Kinley Hall Room 141 | Business Casual Attire"
