Professional

Accounting Club

Accounting Club works to connect students potentially interested in accounting with recruiters from top public accounting firms, finance and internal accounting departments from industry leading companies, and banking/regulatory institutions to help students learn more about what a career in each field looks like, network with experienced professionals, and to hear about both internship and full-time opportunities at these companies. Alongside professional events, Accounting Club hosts both social and philanthropy events to help students get to know other students interested in accounting as well as to give back to the Champaign-Urbana community. Accounting Club is open to all majors (Gies & non-Gies) and there are no essays or interviews involved in becoming a member of the club. Please attend one or both of our info nights listed below to learn more about the club and how to join as well to hear from our corporate partners who will be presenting alongside our club. Info night attendance is not required to become a member, however, it’s a great opportunity to get answers to questions you may have about the club and to hear from top companies that are hiring accounting students.

Meeting: Weekly
Recruitment: "Info Night 1: EY | Wednesday August 26th, 6:00 PM | David Kinley Hall Room 141 | Business Casual Attire Info Night 2: BDO | Thursday August 27th, 6:00 PM | David Kinley Hall Room 141 | Business Casual Attire"

Marketing

American Marketing Association

AMA is the only marketing organization on campus. We provide students with hands-on marketing experience you won’t find in the classroom — and a community of passionate marketers. Through our workshops, guest speakers, panels, and case competitions, students can build real skills while exploring different areas of marketing. We also offer a mentorship program to help members grow both professionally and personally. Members can apply to join one of our two agencies: Consulting Agency – Work on client-facing team projects for local businesses and startups. Marketing Agency – Join our internal marketing team to lead campaigns, content creation, and branding efforts. We’re looking for students who are excited to learn, lead, and collaborate — no prior experience is required. All majors and grade levels are welcome.

Ascend UIUC

Ascend is the largest Pan-Asian interest business organization in North America. Our goal is to foster professional growth and leadership in students by helping them build the skills needed to thrive as leaders in today’s workforce. The UIUC Ascend chapter is driven by the 50 events and activities we host each semester, exposing members to different industries through our career development presentations, connecting members through our social events, and giving back to the community through philanthropy. Ascend UIUC is open to all students of all backgrounds and all majors. Applications: Application and interview is required. Applications are open to undergraduates of all majors. More information on our application/recruitment process can be found on our Instagram! Requirements: Must be an undergraduate.
